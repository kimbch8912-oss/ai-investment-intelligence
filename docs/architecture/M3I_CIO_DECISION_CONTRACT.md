# M3-I CIO Decision Contract

CIO Input prioritizes M2 deterministic snapshot, normalized evidence, structured claims, Risk Context, and Devil challenges. Claims remain inference, not fact. Output stance is a strategic environment posture, never BUY/SELL or allocation. It preserves supporting/counter evidence, disagreement, up to three non-probabilistic scenarios, invalidation conditions, monitoring priorities, and UNKNOWNs. No new score, fact, calculation, or persistence is allowed.
