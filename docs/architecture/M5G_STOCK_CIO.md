# M5-G Stock CIO

M5-G deterministically selects only `STRONG_INTEREST`, `INTEREST`, `NEUTRAL`, `CAUTION`, `HIGH_RISK`, or `UNKNOWN`. It creates no score, price target, allocation, probability, or BUY/SELL instruction. The output states that final investment decisions remain the user's judgment.

Strong interest requires positive technical, fundamental and industry contexts plus positive macro/market and low risk. Interest requires positive stock-specific technical/fundamental/industry contexts. Conflicts cap to neutral; weak core contexts yield caution. High risk cannot produce strong interest and becomes high risk only when all core contexts are weak and macro/market are hostile. Macro/market never rewrite company assessments.

Confidence is mean supplied context confidence; evidence is restricted to the input allow-list. Invalidation and monitoring are generic existing-context review conditions, never price levels. Validators reject evidence fabrication, scope mismatch, recommendations, targets, allocation and probability language.
